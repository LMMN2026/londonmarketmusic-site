Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how London Market Music Network (“LMMN”) uses personal information provided through its pre-launch mailing list.

Information we collect

We collect your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your first name.

Why we use it

We use this information to send updates about LMMN’s launch, events, membership and activities.

Our lawful basis

We rely on your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in our emails.

Our email provider

We use Mailchimp to manage the mailing list and send email updates. Information submitted through the signup form is transferred to and processed by Mailchimp. Mailchimp may process information outside the United Kingdom subject to its applicable data-protection safeguards.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for their own unrelated marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep your mailing-list information while you remain subscribed and may retain limited records where reasonably necessary to demonstrate consent or respect an unsubscribe request.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and request data portability. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Complaints

You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) if you believe your personal information has not been handled properly.

Contact us

For privacy questions, contact hello@lmmn.co.uk.
